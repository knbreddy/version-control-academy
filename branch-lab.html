GUIDED HANDS-ON LAB

Two developers, one file

Alice wants to charge 120. Bob wants to charge 90. Both start from a shared price of 100. Your job is to preserve both histories and resolve the merge with the product owner's approved price: 110.

    Why this matters

    Git can preserve both change histories, but it cannot decide which business value is correct. A conflict is a request for a human decision.
